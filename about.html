01 About

The story
so far.

Born in Zaria. Studying nursing. Learning AI. Writing love stories. Building a clothing business. Still deciding what to leave out.

Chioma Silas was born on the 29th of April in Zaria, Kaduna State, Nigeria, and has been collecting disciplines ever since.

She is a nursing student currently pursuing her university education, while actively exploring and developing skills in technology, entrepreneurship, creativity and business. Nursing is the centre of gravity: it asks for precision, patience and care, and it shapes how she approaches everything else.

She is currently studying AI automation at an academy, where she is learning workflow automation, AI implementation and business process automation using tools such as n8n and Docker. In parallel, she is learning AI video creation, UGC content production, cinematic advertising, prompt engineering and digital content creation.

She is building her personal brand, Oma AI and Tech, while working on YouTube automation and AI-powered content production. Beyond technology, she is a romance fiction writer and ghostwriter, actively developing multiple fiction projects and exploring digital publishing opportunities.

She is also the founder of Omas Empire, a clothing business operating around Katsina and Zaria. Alongside all of this, she is committed to developing her communication, public speaking, leadership and personal productivity skills, while balancing nursing school with technology training, content creation, writing, publishing and entrepreneurship.
