01 Profile

A student of nursing.
A builder of systems.
A writer of love stories.

Chioma Silas is a nursing student, AI automation learner, content creator, romance writer and founder, often all in the same week.

She is pursuing her university education in nursing while training in AI automation at an academy, learning workflow automation, AI implementation and business process automation with tools such as n8n and Docker.

Alongside that, she creates AI video and UGC content, studies cinematic advertising and prompt engineering, and is building her personal brand, Oma AI and Tech, with a focus on YouTube automation and AI-powered production.

She is also a romance fiction writer and ghostwriter, developing multiple fiction projects and exploring digital publishing, and the founder of Omas Empire, a clothing business operating around Katsina and Zaria.
