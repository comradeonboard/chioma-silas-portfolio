04 Writing

Love stories,
written with care.

Romance fiction and ghostwriting: stories about people finding each other, and sometimes themselves, in the middle of an ordinary life.

Underneath the automation, the content and the clothing business, there is a writer who keeps coming back to the page.

Chioma writes romance fiction, stories built on feeling, tension and the small decisions that change a relationship. She is actively developing multiple fiction projects and exploring digital publishing as a route to get them into readers' hands.

She also works as a ghostwriter, carrying another person's voice with care: their tone, their rhythm, their story, written as if it came from them.
